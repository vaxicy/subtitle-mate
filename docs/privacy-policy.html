SubtitleMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026 | 最后更新：2026年8月10日

1. Information We Do Not Collect

SubtitleMate is designed to be privacy-first. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically, we do not track your browsing history, your YouTube watch history, your Google or YouTube account credentials, your IP address for profiling, or any video, subtitle, or translation content you view.

The Extension operates entirely on your device. There is no account to register, no login wall, and no backend database that receives your data. Because no personal data leaves your browser, there is nothing for us to retain, share, or expose.

2. Local Settings Only

Your preferences — such as caption mode (translate or auto-generated), target language, playback speed, and the automatic-reload-on-failure toggle — are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's Storage APIs (chrome.storage.sync and chrome.storage.local). These values stay on your own device.

They are never uploaded to our servers, never sent to third parties, and never used for analytics. If you uninstall the Extension, these local settings are removed together with it. You can also clear them at any time by resetting the options inside the popup.

3. How YouTube Content Is Processed

SubtitleMate runs inside the YouTube page you are visiting. Its only purpose is to read the video player's state and select the appropriate caption track (automatically translating it to your chosen language, or enabling English auto-generated captions). All of this happens locally in your browser tab.

No video frames, audio, subtitle text, or translation results are copied, cached, or transmitted anywhere. The Extension does not take screenshots, does not record, and does not build any profile of the videos you watch. Your viewing activity remains strictly between you and YouTube.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

SubtitleMate does not integrate with any external analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or AI inference services. We do not load third-party scripts, pixels, or trackers.

The only network activity the Extension performs is the standard communication Chrome extensions use to function (for example, reading and writing your local settings through chrome.storage). It does not make outbound requests to any domain other than the YouTube page you are currently on. There is no remote code execution and no data sent to any server we operate.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at huangzero2004@gmail.com. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible.

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in the Extension. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the current version.

1. 我们不收集的信息

SubtitleMate 以隐私优先为原则设计。我们不会收集、存储、传输或出售任何可识别个人身份的信息（PII）。具体而言，我们不会追踪你的浏览历史、YouTube 观看记录、你的 Google 或 YouTube 账号凭据，也不会收集用于画像的 IP 地址，更不会收集你观看的任何视频、字幕或翻译内容。

本扩展完全在你的设备上运行。无需注册账号、没有登录门槛，也没有接收你数据的后端数据库。由于没有任何个人数据离开你的浏览器，我们就没有可保留、共享或泄露的信息。

2. 仅本地保存的设置

你的偏好设置——例如字幕模式（翻译或自动生成）、目标语言、播放速度，以及"应用失败时自动刷新"开关——仅通过 Chrome 的 Storage API（chrome.storage.sync 与 chrome.storage.local）保存在你的浏览器本地，始终留在你自己的设备上。

这些数据永远不会上传到我们的服务器，不会发送给任何第三方，也不会用于分析。当你卸载扩展时，这些本地设置会随之一并清除；你也可以随时在弹窗中重置选项来清空它们。

3. YouTube 内容的处理方式

SubtitleMate 在你正在访问的 YouTube 页面内运行，其唯一目的是读取视频播放器的状态并选择合适的字幕轨道（将其自动翻译为你所选语言，或启用英语"自动生成"字幕）。所有这些操作都在你的浏览器标签页本地完成。

任何视频画面、音频、字幕文本或翻译结果都不会被复制、缓存或传输到任何地方。本扩展不会截图、不会录屏，也不会对你观看的视频建立任何画像。你的观看行为严格只存在于你和 YouTube 之间。

4. 第三方服务与网络请求

SubtitleMate 不接入任何外部数据分析、广告、崩溃上报或 AI 推理服务。我们不加载第三方脚本、追踪像素或跟踪器。

本扩展产生的唯一网络活动，是 Chrome 扩展正常运行所需的标准通信（例如通过 chrome.storage 读写你的本地设置）。除你当前所在的 YouTube 页面外，它不会向任何其他域名发起对外请求。不存在远程代码执行，也没有任何数据被发送到我们运营的任何服务器。
