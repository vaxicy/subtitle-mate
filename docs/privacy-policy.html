SubtitleMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026 | 最后更新：2026年8月10日

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not track your browsing history, video watch history, account credentials, or any content you view.

Local Settings Only

Your preferences (such as caption mode, target language, and playback speed) are stored locally in your browser using Chrome Storage APIs (chrome.storage.sync / chrome.storage.local). These settings remain on your device and are not sent to our servers.

How YouTube Content Is Processed

The Extension runs inside the YouTube page to read the video player state and select caption tracks. All processing happens locally in your browser. No video data, subtitles, or translations are sent to any third-party service or backend server.

Third-Party Services

SubtitleMate does not integrate with any external analytics, advertising, or AI services. The Extension does not make network requests to any domain other than the YouTube page you are currently visiting.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at huangzero2004@gmail.com.

我们不收集的信息

我们不会收集、存储或传输任何可识别个人身份的信息（PII）。我们不会追踪你的浏览历史、观看记录、账号凭据或你查看的任何内容。

仅本地保存设置

你的偏好设置（如字幕模式、目标语言、播放速度等）仅通过 Chrome Storage API（chrome.storage.sync / chrome.storage.local）保存在你的浏览器本地。这些设置不会发送到我们的服务器。

YouTube 内容的处理方式

本扩展在 YouTube 页面内运行，用于读取视频播放器状态并选择字幕轨道。所有处理均在浏览器本地完成。视频数据、字幕文本或翻译结果不会被发送到任何第三方服务或后端服务器。

第三方服务

SubtitleMate 不接入任何外部数据分析、广告或 AI 服务。除你当前访问的 YouTube 页面外，本扩展不会向任何其他域名发起网络请求。
