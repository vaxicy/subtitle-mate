Privacy Policy

SubtitleMate for YouTube is a lightweight Chrome extension that automatically enables YouTube subtitles and picks translated or English auto-generated tracks per your settings.

Data Collection

SubtitleMate does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data. All preferences (language selections, toggle states) are saved locally in your browser via Chrome Storage Sync and are never sent to any server.

Permissions

The extension requests access to https://www.youtube.com/* solely to interact with the YouTube video player and enable captions/translation. No other websites are accessed.

Third Parties

SubtitleMate for YouTube does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or remote code.

Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue on the project repository.

隐私政策

SubtitleMate for YouTube是一款轻量级 Chrome 扩展，用于自动开启 YouTube 字幕，并按你的设置启用翻译字幕或英语（自动生成）字幕。

数据收集

SubtitleMate for YouTube 不会收集、存储或传输任何个人信息或用户数据。所有偏好设置（语言选择、开关状态）均通过 Chrome Storage Sync 保存在你的浏览器本地，不会发送到任何服务器。

权限说明

扩展仅请求访问 https://www.youtube.com/*，以便与 YouTube 视频播放器交互并启用字幕/翻译。不会访问其他网站。

第三方服务

SubtitleMate for YouTube 不使用任何第三方分析、追踪、广告或远程代码。
