=== 中文详细说明 ===

SubtitleMate for YouTube 是一款专注于 YouTube 的轻量观看助手。

打开任意 YouTube 视频，扩展会先固定你的播放速度，再自动开启字幕：

- 翻译字幕：自动将字幕翻译为你设定的目标语言（支持 15 种常用语）；
- 英语（自动生成）：直接启用 YouTube 内置的英语自动识别字幕。

没有字幕轨道的视频会自动跳过字幕步骤，只保留速度调整，不再反复重试。一次设置，全站生效。

主要功能：

- 进入视频先调速、再开字幕（速度独立于字幕）
- 两种字幕模式：翻译 / 英语自动生成
- 15 种目标语言可选
- 固定播放速度（0.25x – 3x）
- 重试次数可自定义（默认 5 次，1–20）
- 设置即时自动保存，本地存储，不收集任何数据

=== English Detailed Description ===

SubtitleMate for YouTube is a lightweight, privacy-first companion for YouTube.

Open any video and it works in two steps:

1. Playback speed first — your preferred rate is applied immediately, whether or not the video has captions.
2. Captions second — if caption tracks exist, they are turned on in your chosen mode:
   • Translate — auto-translates into your target language (15 languages supported).
   • English (auto-generated) — selects YouTube's built-in English auto-transcription track.

Videos with no caption tracks are skipped: SubtitleMate keeps the speed you set and stops trying, so nothing gets spammed. Set once, works on every video.

Features:

- Speed-first: playback speed applies even when there are no captions
- Two caption modes: translate / English auto-generated
- 15 target languages supported
- Playback speed 0.25x – 3x
- Adjustable retry count (default 5, range 1–20)
- Settings auto-save instantly and stay local — nothing collected, nothing uploaded
