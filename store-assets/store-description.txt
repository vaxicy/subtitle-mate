=== 中文详细说明 ===

SubtitleMate for YouTube 是一款轻量观看助手。打开视频即自动开启字幕，按你的设置选择翻译字幕或英语（自动生成）字幕，并固定到预设播放速度。一次设置，全站生效。支持深色模式，设置本地保存，不收集个人数据。

=== English Detailed Description ===

SubtitleMate for YouTube is a lightweight viewing assistant. It auto-enables captions on every video and picks translated or English auto-generated tracks per your settings, snapping playback to your preset speed. Set once, works everywhere. Dark mode supported; preferences stay local, no personal data collected.
