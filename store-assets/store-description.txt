=== 中文详细说明 ===

SubtitleMate for YouTube 是一款专注于 YouTube 的轻量观看助手。

打开任意 YouTube 视频，扩展会按你的偏好优先固定播放速度，然后再为你开启字幕：

- 翻译字幕：自动将字幕翻译为你设定的目标语言（支持 15 种常用语）；
- 英语（自动生成）：直接启用 YouTube 内置的英语自动识别字幕。

如果当前视频没有字幕轨道，扩展会自动跳过字幕步骤，只保留速度调整，不再空转重试。一次设置，全站生效。

主要功能：

- 进入视频自动先调速、再开字幕（速度独立于字幕）
- 两种字幕模式自由切换（翻译 / 英语自动生成）
- 自定义目标语言，支持 15 种常用语
- 固定播放速度（0.25x – 3x）
- 重试次数可自定义（默认 5 次，1–20 范围内）
- 手动更改设置即时自动保存，无需手动点保存
- 深色模式界面，跟随系统主题
- 设置本地保存，隐私优先

所有偏好设置均存储在浏览器本地（chrome.storage.sync），不收集、不上传任何个人数据。

=== English Detailed Description ===

SubtitleMate for YouTube is a lightweight, privacy-first watching companion for YouTube.

Open any YouTube video and SubtitleMate works in two phases:

1. Playback speed first — your preferred rate is applied immediately, regardless of whether the video has any captions.
2. Captions second — if the video exposes caption tracks, SubtitleMate turns them on according to your chosen mode:
   • Translate captions — auto-translates the original track into your target language (15 languages supported: English, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Italian, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Vietnamese).
   • English (auto-generated) — directly selects YouTube's built-in English auto-transcription track.

If a video has no caption tracks at all, SubtitleMate quietly stops trying — it keeps the playback speed you set and never spams the page. Set once, every video on the site.

Main features:

- Speed-first pipeline: playback speed applies even when there are no captions
- Two caption modes: translate / English auto-generated
- 15 target languages supported
- Configurable playback speed (0.25x – 3x)
- Adjustable retry count (default 5, range 1–20)
- Instant auto-save on any manual setting change
- Dark mode UI that follows your system theme
- All preferences stored locally via chrome.storage.sync

Privacy: SubtitleMate does not collect, transmit, or upload any personal data. It only talks to youtube.com to read your own playback state and applies changes via YouTube's own player API. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party servers.
